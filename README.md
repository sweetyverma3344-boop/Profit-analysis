Profit Analysis Dashboard | Data Visualization

📌 Project Overview

The Profit Analysis Dashboard provides a visual overview of business profitability across product categories, individual items, U.S. states, and time periods. It helps identify profitable categories, investigate low-profit items, and understand quarterly profit trends.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze profit across different product categories.

Identify the least profitable items.

Compare profitability across U.S. states.

Track quarterly profit trends over time.

Explore performance by department.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Data Visualization: Interactive charts and geographical mapping.

Dashboard Filtering: Department-based filtering.

The dashboarding software and data preparation tools are not confirmed by the screenshot.

📈 Dashboard Features

Overall Profit by Category: Compares profit across product categories using a horizontal bar chart.

Top 10 Least Profitable Items: Highlights items with the lowest profit values.

Profit by State: Uses a geographical map to visualize profit distribution across U.S. states.

Profit Trend: Displays quarterly profit changes over time, including an overall trend line.

Department Filters: Allows users to explore Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

🔍 Key Insights

Profit varies significantly across the displayed product categories.

Several items show negative profit, highlighting potential areas for investigation.

Profitability differs across U.S. states.

Quarterly profit fluctuates considerably, with both positive and negative periods.

The trend line indicates an overall upward direction in profit during the displayed period.

💡 Recommendations

Investigate low-profit items by reviewing pricing, costs, and discount strategies.

Analyze state-level performance to identify opportunities for improvement.

Examine quarterly fluctuations to understand changes in profitability.

Review department performance to identify areas for potential growth.
